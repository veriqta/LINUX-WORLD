# Security Safety and Legal Boundaries

Security knowledge does not grant authorization. Work only within written scope on systems, accounts and data that are owned or explicitly authorized. Follow applicable law, contract, policy, privacy, employment and evidence requirements.

## Prohibited repository content

- Real passwords, tokens, private keys or recovery codes
- Live internal addresses, customer records or unredacted incidents
- Functional malware or destructive payloads
- Credential theft, persistence deployment or evasion instructions
- Production exploitation steps
- Evidence collected outside authorization

## Safe public examples

Use synthetic identities, documentation domains, private or documentation ranges, sanitized logs, deliberately invalid secrets, short-lived lab certificates and isolated systems. Clearly label simulated incidents.

## Stop conditions

Stop when scope is uncertain, evidence may be legally sensitive, production impact exceeds authorization, recovery access is lost, real secrets appear, or a test crosses into another tenant, account or network. Preserve the state and contact the authorized owner.
