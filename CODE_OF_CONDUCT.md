# Linux World Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Linux World is committed to a professional, respectful, and learning-focused community.

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and constructively
- Discuss ideas and technical evidence instead of attacking people
- Welcome questions asked in good faith
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and perspectives
- Provide actionable feedback
- Protect private and sensitive information
- Follow repository contribution and security policies
- Accept reasonable moderation decisions

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not permitted:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks
- Discrimination or demeaning comments
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated bad-faith arguments
- Publishing another person's private information
- Sharing credentials, secrets, or confidential data
- Plagiarism or misrepresentation of authorship
- Spam, unauthorized promotion, or unrelated solicitation
- Encouraging unsafe, illegal, or malicious system activity
- Retaliation against a person who reports a concern

## Technical disagreements

Technical disagreement is expected. Participants should provide reproducible evidence, relevant logs, version information, authoritative references, or tested examples whenever possible.

Maintainers may close discussions that become repetitive, hostile, unsafe, unrelated, or unsupported by evidence.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces managed by Linux World or VERIQTA. It also applies when a person publicly represents the project.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately through the official [VERIQTA GitHub account](https://github.com/veriqta). Do not disclose sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Reports should include relevant links, dates, context, and supporting material. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves violate this policy.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation.

Enforcement decisions will consider severity, context, repetition, impact, and risk to the community. Privacy will be respected as far as reasonably possible, but complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
