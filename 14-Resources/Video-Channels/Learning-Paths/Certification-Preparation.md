Use videos as demonstrations. Pair every stage with official documentation and hands-on evidence.

| Stage | Video selection | Required evidence |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Confirm current objectives | Notes, commands, verification, and one independent task |

| 4 | Practise without notes | Notes, commands, verification, and one independent task |

| 6 | Review weak objectives | Notes, commands, verification, and one independent task |
| 7 | Perform full mock tasks | Notes, commands, verification, and one independent task |
| 8 | Verify outputs | Notes, commands, verification, and one independent task |
| 9 | Follow exam rules | Notes, commands, verification, and one independent task |
| 10 | Final readiness review | Notes, commands, verification, and one independent task |

## Completion gate

The learner can perform the final task without replaying the video, explain the important decisions, diagnose one controlled failure, and cite the authoritative documentation.
