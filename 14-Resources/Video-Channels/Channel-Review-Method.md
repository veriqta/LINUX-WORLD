# Channel Review Method

## Sample selection

Review the channel description, recent uploads, the recommended playlist, one beginner lesson, one advanced lesson, one security-sensitive procedure, and one troubleshooting video.

## Reproduction

Reproduce representative commands on a disposable system matching the stated distribution. Check prerequisites, outputs, persistent changes, failure handling, cleanup, and rollback.

## Score

Score accuracy, teaching quality, safety, currency, accessibility, transparency, and reproducibility from 0 to 4. Require no zero in accuracy, safety, or legality.

## Record

Document channel owner, URL, classification, subjects, levels, distribution scope, language, captions, activity, playlists, strengths, limitations, verification sources, review date, and next review.
