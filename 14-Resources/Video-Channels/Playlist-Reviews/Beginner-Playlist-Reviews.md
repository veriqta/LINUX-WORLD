# Beginner Playlist Reviews

## Review scope

Linux installation, command line, files, permissions, packages, processes, services, networking, and basic scripting.

## Approved playlist records

No playlist receives permanent approval merely from channel inclusion. Add a record only after completing the full review method and reproduction test.
