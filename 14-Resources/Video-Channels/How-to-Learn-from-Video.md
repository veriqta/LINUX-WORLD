# How to Learn from Video

## Before playback

Write the objective, prerequisites, distribution, version, lab plan, and expected evidence. Open the matching official documentation.

## Active viewing cycle

1. Watch five to fifteen minutes.
2. Pause and predict the next result.
3. Type commands manually after reading their manuals.
4. Explain each important option.
5. Capture expected and actual output.
6. Verify state with a different command.
7. Change one condition and observe the effect.
8. Create one safe failure and recover.
9. Summarize the lesson without replaying it.

## Completion standard

A video is complete only when the learner can reproduce the task, explain the decisions, detect an incorrect result, recover from a controlled failure, and identify the authoritative documentation.

## Never copy blindly

Pause before commands involving `sudo`, deletion, recursive permission changes, raw disks, filesystems, firewall rules, SSH configuration, bootloaders, kernel parameters, or remote scripts.
