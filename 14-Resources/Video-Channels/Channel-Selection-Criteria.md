# Channel Selection Criteria

Review channels for accuracy, instructor authority, command explanation, reproducibility, version labeling, safety, verification, correction history, playlist organization, captions, terminal readability, supporting files, sponsorship disclosure, and legal content.

## Disqualifying patterns

- Examination dumps or leaked questions
- Pirated material or unauthorized reuploads
- Repeated unsafe root commands without warnings
- Disabling security controls as routine troubleshooting
- Hidden sponsorship or deceptive downloads
- Fabricated credentials or guaranteed-job claims
- Content farms that copy documentation without understanding

## Decision

Channel approval never approves all past or future videos. Record recommended playlists, excluded areas, version risks, and the next review date.
