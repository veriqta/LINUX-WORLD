# Book Selection Criteria

Books are reviewed for author expertise, publisher transparency, technical accuracy, organization, depth, exercise quality, safety, edition currency, errata, accessibility, lawful access, and relevance to Linux administration.

Older books may remain recommended for stable concepts such as Unix design, networking principles, filesystems, or performance methodology. Their version-sensitive instructions must be labeled.

Each entry records title, author, publisher, edition, year, level, topics, distribution scope, strengths, limitations, companion material, errata, access type, and review date.
