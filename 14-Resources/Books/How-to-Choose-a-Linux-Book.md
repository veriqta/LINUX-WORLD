# How to Choose a Linux Book

1. Define the outcome: foundations, administration, certification, internals, performance, security, or production operations.
2. Match the difficulty to current skill, not job title.
3. Check the edition and the age-sensitive parts, including package tools, network configuration, containers, and certification objectives.
4. Preview the table of contents and one technical chapter.
5. Confirm exercises, companion material, errata, and legal access.
6. Pair the book with a lab and official documentation.

## Active reading

For every chapter, write learning objectives, reproduce examples, change one condition, capture failure evidence, explain the result, and record questions for further research.
