# Resource Selection Standard

This standard governs every external resource listed in Linux World.

## Mandatory admission checks

A resource must have a clear owner, lawful availability, direct relevance to Linux system administration, and identifiable educational value. Reviewers must be able to determine its version scope and must not find instructions that create unreasonable risk without warnings or rollback guidance.

## Scoring model

Score each category from 0 to 4. A resource normally requires at least 24 of 32 points and no zero in accuracy, legality, or safety.

| Category | 0 | 2 | 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Materially wrong | Mostly correct, gaps present | Verified against primary sources |
| Authority | Unknown owner | Experienced community source | Official or demonstrably expert source |
| Educational value | No clear outcome | Useful explanation | Structured, reproducible learning |
| Safety | Unsafe | Warnings incomplete | Risks, checks, rollback, verification |
| Freshness | Obsolete | Mixed versions | Current or explicitly timeless |
| Accessibility | Major barriers | Partly accessible | Clear, navigable, captions or text alternatives |
| Transparency | Hidden interests | Some disclosures | Ownership, cost, sponsorship, limits clear |
| Maintainability | Unstable link | Reviewable | Canonical URL and update history |

## Automatic rejection

- Pirated books, courses, software, or unauthorized mirrors
- Examination dumps, leaked questions, or claims of guaranteed certification
- Malware, credential theft, cryptomining, or deceptive downloads
- Commands presented for production use without necessary risk context
- Fabricated credentials, authorship, reviews, or performance claims
- Hidden affiliate redirection or undisclosed paid placement
- Scraped material that removes attribution or licensing information
- Sources whose central purpose falls outside Linux administration

## Conditional inclusion

Older material may be included when it teaches stable concepts. It must state the original version, identify obsolete procedures, provide a current alternative, and carry an archived or historical label.

## Independence

Popularity, subscriber count, search ranking, employer, nationality, or production quality cannot substitute for technical review. Contributors must disclose financial, employment, or personal relationships with a recommended source.
