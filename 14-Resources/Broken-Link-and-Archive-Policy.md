# Broken Link and Archive Policy

This policy preserves useful history without presenting obsolete material as current guidance.

## Broken-link response

1. Confirm the failure from a clean session.
2. Check the publisher's canonical site for a moved page.
3. Update redirects only when ownership and content remain consistent.
4. Search official release notes or project indexes.
5. Mark the entry unavailable when no trustworthy replacement exists.
6. Add a replacement or archive record.

Do not replace an official source with an arbitrary mirror.

## Archive criteria

Archive material when its covered release is unsupported, commands rely on removed interfaces, ownership is uncertain, a better current source replaces it, or the publisher no longer maintains it.

## Required archive notice

Every archived record states the archive date, reason, original scope, known unsafe or obsolete instructions, current replacement, and whether the material remains useful for history or concepts.

## Web archives

Archived snapshots may support historical research. They must not bypass payment, access control, licensing, or removal requests. Never treat an archived copy as proof that an old procedure remains supported.
