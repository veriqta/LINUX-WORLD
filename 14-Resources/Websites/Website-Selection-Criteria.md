# Website Selection Criteria

Websites are reviewed for identifiable authorship, technical accuracy, reproducibility, update history, stable URLs, legal access, accessible presentation, safe command practices, and value beyond existing Linux World material.

## Additional checks

- Does the site distinguish distributions and versions?
- Are corrections and dates visible?
- Are downloads served from trustworthy locations with integrity information?
- Are commercial relationships clear?
- Can important claims be verified through primary sources?
- Does the site remain useful without intrusive registration or tracking?

Sites that publish useful historical material may be archived with explicit warnings.
