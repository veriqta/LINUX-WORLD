## Use checklist

- Identify the author, owner, update date, and covered version.
- Compare critical claims with official documentation.
- Reproduce commands in a lab and inspect every option.
- Record access, security, cost, and availability limitations.
- Report moved, abandoned, compromised, or paywalled resources.
