# Certificate and Secret Lifecycle Platform: Prerequisites

Complete relevant Junior and Mid-Level projects. Understand Linux internals, systemd, networking, storage, identity, cgroup v2, security policy, automation, observability, incident command, recovery objectives, and architecture decisions.

Use an isolated multi-host lab. Record scale assumptions, versions, topology, resources, dependencies, trust anchors, time source, and recovery access. Use only synthetic data and disposable credentials.
