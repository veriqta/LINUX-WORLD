# Fleet Bootstrap and Compliance: Production Requirements

## Objectives

Define availability, latency, correctness, RPO, RTO, capacity headroom, detection time, mitigation time, and change-failure rate.

## Controls

- Strong workload and operator identity
- Least privilege and separated duties
- Signed or checksummed artifacts
- Progressive delivery and rollback
- Resource and queue limits
- Telemetry-loss detection
- Protected evidence and audit
- Tested backup and recovery
- Time-bound exceptions
- Owned residual risks

## Readiness gates

No production-readiness claim is allowed without long-duration evidence, representative load, failure testing, recovery exercises, security review, ownership, and documented limitations.
