# Performance Baseline and Capacity Study: Prerequisites

## Required foundation

Complete relevant Junior projects and understand systemd, permissions, networking, logs, Bash, package management, and troubleshooting.

## Lab

Use two or more disposable VMs, containers, or namespaces when the project has clients, servers, failover, or collection. Record OS versions, addresses, names, resources, time source, and installed packages.

## Safety

Never target production. Snapshot systems before privileged work. Keep console access during SSH, firewall, storage, or resolver changes. Use synthetic data and disposable credentials.
