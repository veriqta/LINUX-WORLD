# Highly Available Reverse Proxy: Interview Questions

1. What problem and user journey does the project serve?
2. Where are control, data, and state?
3. Which trust boundaries exist?
4. What is the first saturation point?
5. How is bad configuration stopped?
6. How is access limited and revoked?
7. Which failure was hardest to diagnose?
8. What evidence distinguished cause from symptom?
9. What are rollback triggers?
10. How are recovery and data integrity verified?
11. What would change at fleet scale?
12. Which limitation prevents a production-readiness claim?
