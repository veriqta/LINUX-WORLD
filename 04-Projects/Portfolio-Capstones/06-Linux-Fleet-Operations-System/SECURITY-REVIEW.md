# Linux Fleet Operations System: Security Review

## Required review areas

- Assets and threat actors
- Trust boundaries and attack paths
- Administrative and workload identity
- Least privilege and separation of duties
- Secret and certificate lifecycle
- Network and tenant isolation
- Supply-chain integrity
- Host hardening and patching
- Audit integrity and evidence access
- Backup protection and destructive recovery risk
- Emergency access and revocation
- Vulnerability and incident response

## Required tests

Test approved access, denied access, expired or revoked credentials, malformed input, unauthorized network path, tampered artifact, audit loss, backup integrity, and emergency isolation.

Record residual risks with owner, severity, compensating control, decision, and expiry.
