# Multi-Site Operations Simulation: SLO and Capacity Plan

## Define

- Critical user journeys
- Availability and latency objectives
- Error-budget window
- Correctness and durability indicators
- RPO and RTO
- Expected and peak workload
- CPU, memory, I/O, network, storage, connections, and queue limits
- Capacity headroom and scaling trigger

## Required evidence

Measure baseline, peak, saturation point, recovery behavior, backlog drain, noisy-neighbor impact, telemetry cost, backup duration, restore duration, and forecast assumptions.

Do not claim scale from a short synthetic test. State exactly what the lab proves.
