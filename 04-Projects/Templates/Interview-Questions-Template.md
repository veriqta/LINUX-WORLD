# {{PROJECT_NAME}}: Interview Questions

Answer from personal implementation evidence. Do not memorize a reference answer without understanding it.

## Explain the system

1. What problem did the project solve, and how did you define success?
2. Walk through the architecture and each trust boundary.
3. Which component owns state, and why?
4. Which decision had the strongest alternative?

## Operate and troubleshoot

5. What signals prove health from a user's perspective?
6. Describe a failure you injected, the evidence used, and recovery.
7. What would you check before restarting the service?
8. How do backup and restore meet RPO and RTO?

## Security and growth

9. Where does privilege change, and how is least privilege enforced?
10. How are secrets stored, delivered, rotated, and kept out of logs?
11. Which risk remains accepted, and who owns it?
12. What breaks first at ten times the workload?
13. How would the design change for multiple regions or tenants?
14. Which manual step would you automate next, and what risk would it add?
15. What did this project not prove?
