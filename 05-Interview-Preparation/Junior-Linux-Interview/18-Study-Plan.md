# Junior Study Plan

## Eight-week plan

| Week | Focus | Required proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Foundations and environment | Baseline report and command journal |
| 2 | Files, identity and permissions | Least-privilege lab with tests |
| 3 | Processes, services and boot | Failed-service investigation |
| 4 | Packages, storage and persistence | Rebuild and rollback evidence |
| 5 | Networking, DNS and remote access | Packet-path troubleshooting report |
| 6 | Logs, performance and security | Evidence timeline and hardening review |
| 7 | Automation, backup and recovery | Tested script and restore exercise |
| 8 | Mock interviews and practical exam | Two timed attempts and gap review |

## Daily practice

1. Explain one concept without notes.
2. Run one read-only inspection and interpret every important field.
3. Make one reversible lab change.
4. Trigger one bounded failure.
5. Recover and verify.
6. Write one interview answer from evidence.

## Readiness gate

Achieve at least 80 percent twice under timed conditions, with no critical safety failure. Revisit weak domains by rebuilding them, not by memorizing answers.
