# Linux Foundation Certified System Administrator: Study and Exam Strategy

## Preparation loop

1. Read the current official objective verbatim.
2. Explain it without notes.
3. Implement it on a clean supported system.
4. Verify through a second tool or user path.
5. Introduce a bounded failure and diagnose it.
6. Restore and confirm persistence.
7. Repeat under time pressure.

## Exam discipline

Read the full task, preserve requested names and paths, validate targets, avoid changing unrelated state, use local documentation only when permitted, check every final condition and reserve time for a complete review.

## Readiness standard

Score at least 85 percent on two unseen mocks and complete practical objectives twice without critical safety mistakes. Official pass rules belong to the vendor and may differ.
