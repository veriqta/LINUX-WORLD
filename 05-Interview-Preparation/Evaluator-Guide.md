# Evaluator Guide

## Evaluate the declared level

Junior candidates need safe fundamentals and good escalation. Mid-Level candidates should independently diagnose and operate services. Senior candidates must manage ambiguity, architecture, fleet risk and incident leadership. Do not use Senior trivia to reject a credible Junior candidate.

## Scoring dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Accuracy and mechanism | 25 | Explanation matches Linux behavior |
| Investigation and evidence | 20 | Hypotheses are tested with relevant signals |
| Practical execution | 20 | Required state is reached reproducibly |
| Safety and security | 15 | Target, privilege, secrets and rollback are controlled |
| Verification and recovery | 10 | End-to-end and negative checks pass |
| Communication and ownership | 10 | Assumptions, uncertainty and decisions are clear |

## Fairness controls

State the distribution and available tools. Allow equivalent commands. Separate memory from reasoning. Do not require proprietary knowledge absent from the role. Offer accessible alternatives when a task format creates an unrelated barrier. Record evidence for each score.

## Automatic concerns

Dishonesty, unauthorized access, exposed secrets, reckless destructive action, deliberate disabling of security without justification, or inability to recognize a dangerous command requires explicit review.
