# Technical Diagrams

Diagrams explain structures, flows, dependencies, decisions, states, and failure paths that are difficult to understand from prose alone.

## Source and export rule

Store editable originals in `source/` and published renderings in `exported/`. Topic directories provide discovery guidance and may hold topic-specific source and exports when a single central source directory becomes difficult to maintain. Do not keep two editable masters for one asset.

## Suitable diagram types

- Architecture and component maps
- Flows and timelines
- State transitions
- Layer and stack views
- Decision trees
- Trust boundaries and threat models
- Network and lab topologies
- Failure propagation and recovery sequences

Every complex diagram requires a text equivalent in the related document.
