# Licensing and Attribution

Every asset must have known ownership and permitted use. Repository licensing does not override third-party copyright, trademark, or attribution conditions.

## Asset ownership classes

- **Original:** Created specifically for Linux World.
- **Contributor-owned:** Submitted by a contributor with the required permission.
- **Third-party licensed:** Used under a documented license.
- **Permission granted:** Used under written permission with recorded scope.
- **Trademarked identifier:** Used only under the owner's applicable trademark rules.

## Required record

Record the asset ID, title, creator, copyright owner, original URL, license name and version, license URL, required attribution, modifications, permission scope, repository locations, and review date.

## Do not assume

Public availability, search-engine visibility, a missing copyright notice, or educational use does not automatically grant redistribution rights. Do not copy diagrams, book pages, course slides, screenshots, logos, mascots, or icons without a documented basis.

## Third-party marks

Distribution and product logos remain the property of their owners. Do not recolor, distort, combine, imply endorsement, or treat them as Linux World branding. Follow the owner's current trademark and logo-use guidance.

## Contribution review

Contributors must disclose whether they created the asset, derived it from another work, used a template, used generative tools, or received direct permission. Unclear ownership blocks publication.

This document defines repository controls and is not legal advice. Questions about uncertain rights should be escalated before publication.
