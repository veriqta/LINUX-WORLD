# Lab Selection Guide

## Select by learning need

| Need | Choose |
| --- | --- |
| Learn a new skill with explanation | Guided Lab |
| Prove a recently learned skill | Independent Lab |
| Combine domains and make decisions | Challenge Lab |
| Practise diagnosis and recovery | Failure Injection Lab |
| Operate under realistic scope and pressure | Production Simulation |

## Select by level

| Level | Guidance | Expected behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Junior | Detailed steps and frequent checkpoints | Execute safely, explain commands, verify and escalate |
| Mid-Level | Requirements, constraints and partial guidance | Plan, investigate, decide, automate and own recovery |
| Senior | Business scenario, objectives and operational limits | Design controls, manage risk, lead response and defend trade-offs |

## Readiness gates

Move from Guided to Independent after two clean completions and an explanation of the underlying state changes. Move to Challenge after passing independent acceptance tests without solution access. Begin Failure Injection only after proving reset and recovery. Begin Production Simulations after completing at least one challenge and one failure lab in each relevant domain.

## Domain routes

- New administrators: foundations, identity, permissions, packages, processes, systemd, storage and networking.
- Service administrators: deployment, logs, timers, backup, firewall, hardening and monitoring.
- Production operators: performance, capacity, multi-node networking, incident response and recovery.
- Technical leads: fleet change, reliability objectives, migration, disaster recovery and incident command.
