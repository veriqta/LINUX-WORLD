# Notebook Quality Checklist

## Before work

- [ ] Correct notebook and scope selected.
- [ ] Record ID, owner, UTC time, environment and classification completed.
- [ ] Exact targets and authorization verified.
- [ ] Before state, dependencies, risk, stop conditions and recovery path recorded.

## During work

- [ ] Facts, interpretations, hypotheses, decisions and actions are distinguishable.
- [ ] Commands identify target, identity, purpose, exit status and relevant output.
- [ ] Evidence is timestamped, attributable, minimal and integrity-protected when required.
- [ ] Deviations and failed tests are retained, not rewritten out of history.

## Closeout

- [ ] Primary, negative, security, dependency, persistence and monitoring checks completed as applicable.
- [ ] Final state and comparison with success criteria recorded.
- [ ] Rollback performed or its readiness verified.
- [ ] Remaining risk, follow-ups, owners and due dates assigned.
- [ ] Sensitive data review and link validation completed.
- [ ] Required reviewer and approver decisions recorded.

If a check is not applicable, record why. Blank fields are not evidence.
