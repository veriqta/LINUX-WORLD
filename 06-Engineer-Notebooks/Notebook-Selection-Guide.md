# Notebook Selection Guide

Choose the record that best matches the decision or operational outcome.

| Need | Start with |
| --- | --- |
| Routine host check, inventory or simple authorized change | A Junior notebook |
| Cross-component investigation, deployment, recovery or service ownership | A Mid-Level notebook |
| Architecture, fleet risk, reliability policy, major incident or governance | A Senior notebook |
| One small record embedded in another workflow | A Shared Template |
| Study a finished public example | A Completed Example |

## Selection rules

- Prefer the narrowest notebook that captures the work.
- Link related records instead of copying their full contents.
- Use an incident notebook for coordination and a specialized investigation notebook for deep technical analysis.
- Use a change record for execution and an architecture decision record for a durable design choice.
- Escalate the record level when scope, uncertainty, reversibility or impact increases.

The level describes the work, not the job title of the author.
